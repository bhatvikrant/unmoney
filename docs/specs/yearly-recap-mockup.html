2 · The recap (tap to step through)

Tap right to continue · left to go back · ← → on a keyboard

Same bones as the monthly recap: lead-in, one big thing, a visual that fills the middle, a footnote. The year adds four cards the month cannot carry: the calendar (every day of the year as a cell), the top five, on the clock (when in the day you spend, only if times are logged), and your spending personality, an archetype chosen by fixed rules from the year's numbers, with the three figures that decided it shown underneath so it never feels like a guess.

Up to 14 cards; the ones that need a previous year, logged times, income or budgets are skipped. The run is still under a minute.

4 · Spending personalities

One archetype per year, picked by the first rule that matches, top to bottom. Each comes with the figures behind it. Names are affectionate, never a verdict.

The PlannerScheduled entries (repeats) are 35% or more of spend, or every budget held in 9+ months
The HomebodyHousehold (rent, bills, home) is 40% or more of spend
The FoodieFood and eating out are 30% or more of spend
The ExplorerTravel and transport are 20% or more of spend
The Weekender45% or more of spend lands on Saturdays and Sundays
The Night Owl40% or more of entries after 9 pm (needs times on at least half the entries)
The Big-Day SpenderThe ten largest entries are 40% or more of the year
The Steady HandNo month strays more than 15% from the year's monthly average
The All-RounderNone of the above: spend is spread across categories, days and months
