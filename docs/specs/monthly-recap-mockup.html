2 · The recap (tap to step through)

Tap right to continue · left to go back · ← → on a keyboard

One idea per card: a small uppercase lead-in, one big number, one sentence of context. Nothing scrolls. Cards that don't apply are skipped (no income recorded, no previous month, no budgets), so the recap is 6–9 cards and under 30 seconds.

The numbers come from the same functions as the Stats tab, so the recap total always equals the month's Expenses figure on Stats.

Colour: each card carries one of the chart hues as a faint tint, in the order the categories use on Stats. Switch to "Plain cards" above to see it without.
